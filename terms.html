CLIPClip Poster

Clip Poster is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It records livestream highlights, adds captions, and posts them to the owner's own TikTok and Instagram accounts after the owner approves each one.

What Clip Poster is

Clip Poster is a desktop program for a single user. It is not a public service, and nobody can sign up for it. It is used only by its owner to post short clips to accounts the owner controls.

Use of TikTok and Instagram

Clip Poster connects to TikTok and Instagram only through their official APIs and only with the owner's permission, which the owner gives on TikTok's and Meta's own login screens. The owner can remove that permission at any time in their TikTok or Instagram account settings.

Content

  • The owner is responsible for every clip that is posted and approves each one before it goes out.
  • Clips must follow TikTok's Community Guidelines, Instagram's Community Guidelines, and the rules of the streams they come from.
  • Clips are removed if a streamer or rights holder asks.

No warranty

Clip Poster is provided as is, without any warranty. It may stop working if TikTok, Instagram, or a streaming site changes how it works.
